SHUMSHUFER DESIGN SYSTEM

Driving education, connected.

A trustworthy, modern and practical design system for ShumShufer — an Ethiopian driving-education marketplace, learning platform, school LMS and regulatory ecosystem. This version keeps the clean, structured presentation style of the supplied reference HTML while replacing its visual language with ShumShufer's approved blue/cyan brand system.

Color Palette

Trustworthy blue, supported by clear status colors.

ShumShufer's approved palette uses blue for primary actions and links, cyan for secondary emphasis, and distinct success, warning and danger colors for payment, enrollment and system states. The canvas stays light and calm so the marketplace and learning information remain easy to scan.

Buttons

Clear actions, predictable states.

Primary actions use solid blue. Secondary actions use a clean white surface with a border. Tertiary actions use an outlined blue treatment. The system also reserves red for destructive actions and muted treatments for disabled states.

button-primary Primary decision/action: discovery, enrollment, learning and payment.
button-secondary Secondary action on a white surface.
button-tertiary Lower-priority outlined action.
destructive Use danger only for destructive or cancellation actions.

Cards & Containers

Information-dense without feeling crowded.

Cards use a white surface, 1px border and 12–16px radius. The component system distinguishes schools, courses, learning resources, payments and role-based administration.

school-card

Find a trusted school.

school-lms

admin-card

Operate the ecosystem.

School and government dashboards use the same core components with role-specific permissions.

Form Elements

Short, familiar and mobile-friendly.

Forms should be progressive and easy for first-time digital users. Labels are sentence case, controls have comfortable touch targets, and validation should always explain what happened next.

Spacing Scale

A 4px rhythm from micro gaps to hero space.

The base spacing unit is 4px. Common screen padding is 16–24px, major sections use 24–32px, and hero/display areas use 48px or more.

Elevation & Depth

Use contrast first, shadow only when useful.

White cards on the light canvas establish hierarchy. Borders clarify boundaries. Shadows are reserved for meaningful elevation such as drawers, menus and overlays.

Level 0 — CanvasDefault app background with no unnecessary decoration.
Level 1 — Hairline1px border for inputs, cards and clear component boundaries.
Level 2 — ElevatedSubtle shadow for meaningful overlays and elevated panels.

Responsive Behavior

Mobile-first for learners, responsive for administration.

The student experience prioritizes mobile usage, while school and government portals adapt to larger screens. Navigation, cards and filters collapse progressively without hiding the primary action.
